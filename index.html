MoneyScreen Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app "MoneyScreen - Money Wallpaper" (package com.moneyscreen.app) by Arnas Apps. Effective 26 September 2026.

Nothing leaves your phone. MoneyScreen has no server, no account and no internet permission.

We collect nothing. The developer never receives your numbers, your pictures or any usage data.

You type everything. The app does not read messages, notifications, bank apps or any other app.

What the app stores, and where

MoneyScreen is a wallpaper app. You type the figures you want to see (account balances, this month's credits and debits, EMIs, upcoming payments, expenses and free-form notes) and the app draws them on your lock screen and home screen.

  • Everything you type is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. No other app can read it.
  • A copy of the current wallpaper content is kept in the app's private preferences so the wallpaper service and the home-screen widgets can draw it.
  • If you choose a background picture, the app copies that one picture into its private storage. On Android 12 and older it can copy your current wallpaper for this; on newer versions you pick a picture through the Android photo picker, which gives the app access to that picture only.
  • If you set an app lock PIN, only a salted hash of the PIN is stored, never the PIN itself. Biometric unlock is handled by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app declares no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data.
  • It does not read SMS, notifications, contacts, location, or other apps.
  • It does not link to banks, wallets or payment services, and it never asks for credentials.
  • It contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It gives no financial advice. It only displays what you typed.

Permissions

  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): optional, only if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock for the app lock.
  • Live wallpaper service: so Android can show MoneyScreen as your wallpaper.
  • Quick Settings tile: an optional tile to show or hide the numbers from the lock screen.
  • Home-screen widget: optional widgets that show the same content.

Backups you create

Settings offers "Backup & restore". Export writes one JSON file containing your typed numbers and hands it to the Android share sheet. Where that file goes (your Files app, a cloud drive, an email) is your choice and outside this app. The app never uploads it by itself.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → "Clear all numbers" removes every figure you typed.
  • Settings → Background → "Remove" deletes the copied picture.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. The app's behaviour described above (no collection, no transmission) is a design principle, not a setting.
